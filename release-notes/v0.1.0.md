## First release

freewheel turns a Blackmagic DaVinci Resolve Speed Editor into a macro pad for everything else you do on your computer. While DaVinci Resolve is open, freewheel steps aside so Resolve can use the Speed Editor as usual.

### What you can do

* Set any key to press a shortcut, hold a key while you hold it, turn a key on and off, open an app, or switch to another page of keys.
* Use the wheel to scroll, step through actions, or change the system volume, an app's volume, or your screen brightness.
* Keep several pages of keys and switch between them from the Speed Editor.
* See what each key does on a drawing of the Speed Editor, with its lights showing what is on.
* Connect by USB or Bluetooth, with the battery level shown for Bluetooth.
* Back up your keys to a file and load them on another computer.
* Start freewheel when you log in, and switch it on or off from the tray or menu bar.
* Try everything without a Speed Editor: `freewheel --simulate` opens a simulated one.

### Download

freewheel is portable: there is nothing to install. Download the zip for Windows 11 or macOS 15 (Apple Silicon and Intel), unzip it, and run it. Your settings are kept in your user folder, so moving or replacing the app keeps them.

### Updates

freewheel checks for a new version when it starts, or when you click **Check for updates**. It downloads the update, checks that it is genuine, and installs it when you restart. What's new then shows what changed.
